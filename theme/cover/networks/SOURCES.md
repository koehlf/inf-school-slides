## Cover variants

The cover uses lossless `*-gray.png` derivatives of the nine JPEG originals,
converted with ImageMagick (`magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray output-gray.png`).
The original colour images remain unchanged. The theme tint is applied in
`00_title.tex` with a 30% opaque `borderColor` overlay, matching the other scripts.

`router.jpg` was added by the author without a source. On 2026-10-15 it was
matched (SIFT feature matching) to Pixabay photo 1861612, so it is covered by the
record above; the local file is the 640 px preview.
